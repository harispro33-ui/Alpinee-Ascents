Key Milestones & History

1986 — Founding

Todd Burleson established the company to focus on safe, well-planned international expeditions, careful guide-to-client ratios, and structured itineraries.

Early Himalayan Successes

The company built its reputation on strict safety standards and logistics during early expeditions in the Himalayas.

1992 — Everest Operations

Alpine Ascents International began operating regular commercial expeditions to Mount Everest, eventually guiding over 280 climbers to the summit.

1996 — Everest Disaster

The team played a vital supportive role in rescue efforts during the infamous 1996 Everest storm, emerging from the tragedy with an even stronger emphasis on safety culture.

Expansion

Over four decades, the company grew into a Seven Summits operator, offering over 30 international expeditions and numerous mountaineering training courses in the Pacific Northwest, such as on Mount Rainier and Mount Baker.
