Welcome to Alpine Ascents

Celebrating 40 years of leading successful expeditions in 2026.

Experience. Leadership. Adventure.

Alpine Ascents International, Inc. is a leader in the climbing industry and has been instrumental in setting guiding standards in the international climbing community for most of our history.

This year, we offer more than 30 different expeditions and 50 training courses in 14 countries, including the Arctic and Antarctic regions.

Our philosophy is based on a true love and understanding of mountain wilderness.

Our mission is simple: by using the best climbing talent in the country, we offer climbers at all levels the opportunity to achieve their personal aspirations.
