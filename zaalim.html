We believe that helping climbers achieve their personal goals starts well before the climb itself.

OUR MISSION

DEVELOPING SELF-RELIANT MOUNTAINEERS

Alpine Ascents is committed to developing risk-conscious, self-reliant and environmentally-conscious mountaineers while offering courses and expeditions throughout the world.

OUR HISTORY

A HISTORY OF MOUNTAIN EXPLORATION

Alpine Ascents was founded in 1986 and developed from a focus on international guiding and carefully planned mountain expeditions.

Founder Todd Burleson developed an approach centered around thoughtful route planning, camps, guide ratios, supplies and itineraries.

From early Himalayan expeditions, this approach expanded to mountains around the world.

Today Alpine Ascents continues to operate climbing programs and expeditions in mountain environments around the globe.
